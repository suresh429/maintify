Features

Everything your community needs

Maintify brings all apartment management tasks into a single, easy-to-use platform.

💰

Bill Management

Create monthly bills with smart split options — equal, hybrid, or individual. Track payments, send reminders, and see who's paid.

📋

Complaint System

Residents can raise maintenance complaints with full message threads. Presidents manage and resolve tickets efficiently.

📅

Meeting Management

Schedule society meetings, send automatic notifications, and keep all residents informed and engaged.

👥

Member Directory

Searchable member list with flat numbers, contact details, and resident type — all in one place.

🔔

Push Notifications

Real-time alerts for new bills, complaint updates, payment verifications, and meeting announcements.

📊

Dashboard & Reports

At-a-glance stats for presidents — pending payments, open complaints, upcoming meetings, and flat occupancy.

Role-Based Access

Built for everyone in your community

Three distinct roles keep the right controls in the right hands.

Super Admin

Platform Administrator

Manage all apartments, assign presidents, configure ads, and oversee the entire platform from a single dashboard.

President

Apartment President

Create bills, manage residents, resolve complaints, schedule meetings, and transfer leadership when needed.

Resident

Flat Resident

View bills, mark payments, raise complaints, attend meetings, and stay connected with the community board.
